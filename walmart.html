The Walmart opportunity

Position Walmart as the Total Wellness Destination.

The barrier isn't assortment, it's perception. Walmart's wellness ecosystem (pharmacy, vision, supplements, beauty, fitness tech) already rivals specialty retailers. This moment repositions Walmart beyond value: it puts better-for-you products in shoppers' hands at the January reset and routes every trial back to store.

Why January

No one is more open to something new than they are on January 1.

Coming out of the holidays, households reset. They rethink what they eat, drink, and buy, and they go actively hunting for products that fit the new version of themselves. Sampling in this window doesn't just earn a taste. It lands your product at the exact moment a routine is being rebuilt from scratch.

Peak reset mindset

You reach people the moment they're deciding what belongs in their new routine, and actively swapping old habits for new ones.

Committed, high-frequency

The partnership

A partnership with the gyms & studios where the new year happens.

Running the first weeks of January

A category-exclusive New Year program to bring to your brands: a national network of fitness studios, university housing, and apartments & condos near Walmart locations, where committed members rebuild the new year's routine. Every program spans four connected media products:

  • Category-exclusive: one brand per category, per moment. Claim it and your competitors are locked out.
  • Product sampling: your product handed over by the studio's own instructors, a recommendation from someone members already trust, not an interruption.
  • Email: the studios send your brand to their member lists.
  • Organic social: venues create and post authentic content featuring your product.
  • Amplified media: paid media extends the moment to these shoppers and commerce audiences.
